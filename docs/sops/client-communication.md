# SOP: Client Communication

## Principles

1. **Proactive over reactive** — Tell clients what's happening before they ask
2. **Clear over comprehensive** — One clear sentence beats three ambiguous ones
3. **Action-oriented** — Every communication ends with a clear next step
4. **Honest always** — Bad news delivered early is better than bad news delivered late

## Communication Channels

| Type | Channel | Owner | Frequency |
|------|---------|-------|-----------|
| Status updates | Email | Account Lead | Weekly |
| Quick questions | Slack/Teams | Account Lead | Async |
| Decision needed | Email + Calendar | Account Lead | As needed |
| Escalation | Call | Account Lead | Immediately |

## Status Report Cadence

Weekly status email (every Friday):
- Run `/client-report`
- Account Lead reviews and personalizes
- Sent by EOD Friday

## Escalation Protocol

**When to escalate immediately (same day):**
- Missed deadline
- Scope change that affects timeline or budget
- Critical bug in production
- Team blocker that affects delivery

**When to escalate within 24 hours:**
- Risk identified that may affect delivery
- Decision needed that's been open > 3 days
- Quality concern that may require rework

**Escalation format:**
```
SITUATION: What happened
IMPACT: What this affects
OPTIONS: 2-3 ways we can respond
RECOMMENDATION: What we recommend and why
DECISION NEEDED BY: [date/time]
```

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Acknowledge every message within 24 hours
- Cc: relevant team member on technical questions
- Confirm verbal agreements in writing
- Document decisions

**Don't:**
- Make delivery commitments without team input
- Use technical jargon without explanation
- Send bad news on a Friday afternoon
- CC the client on internal team discussions
