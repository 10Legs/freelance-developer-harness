## The Challenge

Acme Corp's current storefront is a 7-year-old PHP monolith. Page load times average 4.2 seconds. The checkout flow has 8 steps. Mobile conversion is 60% below desktop. The engineering team spends 40% of their time on maintenance rather than new features.

## The Opportunity

A modern storefront built on current tooling would cut load times below 1 second, reduce checkout to 3 steps, and give the marketing team CMS control — freeing engineering to build product features.

## Target Users

Acme's customers are 25–45 year olds purchasing home goods online. They are mobile-first (65% of traffic), price-conscious, and comparison-shopping across multiple tabs. Checkout friction is the #1 dropout reason per their existing analytics.

## Success Criteria

1. Page load time under 1 second (Lighthouse performance score ≥ 90)
2. Checkout abandonment rate reduced by 30% within 60 days of launch
3. Marketing team can publish new content without an engineering ticket

## Out of Scope

- Mobile app (iOS/Android) — future phase
- Inventory management system migration
- Customer loyalty/rewards program

## Brand Considerations

Acme has an existing brand guide (colors, typography, tone). All design must conform to it. Brand guide provided by Priya Nair in Figma.

## Technical Considerations

- Must integrate with existing Shopify Storefront API for inventory and orders
- Existing customer accounts in Shopify — SSO or account migration required
- Payment via Stripe (existing account, just needs new frontend integration)
- Analytics: Google Analytics 4
